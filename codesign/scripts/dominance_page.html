Claims the data supports so far

Configurations

Method. Tasks are paired by seed and task id, so every config saw the same arrivals and tool delays. For each pair the curve is Qcol(p) − Qrow(p) on p = 0.10, 0.15, …, 0.90, pooled over seeds. The band comes from a two-level bootstrap (resample seeds, then tasks within each seed, keeping the pairing), widened to hold over all 17 percentiles at once (sup-t). Tails beyond p10 and p90 are left out because they rest on a handful of tasks.

Verdicts. Dominates: the band stays at or above zero at every percentile. Faster in part: significantly faster at some percentiles and never significantly slower. Crossing: significantly faster at some and significantly slower at others. The darker outline marks verdicts that also hold with a 99% band.

Read with care. Only two seeds per cell so far, so seed-to-seed variation is barely sampled, and a repeat run of the same seed has moved the mean by about 2.7 s. The page makes about 1,000 comparisons, so a few 95% verdicts will be false; trust the ones that hold at 99% or repeat across memory sizes and task classes. Generated by codesign/scripts/dominance.py.
